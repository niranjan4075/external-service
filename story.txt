### **Description**

Enable Mobile Device Management (MDM) support within Storefront by introducing mobile devices into the catalog and defining the end-to-end request and approval workflow. This feature focuses on making mobile devices available to users based on their role, along with proper routing, visibility, and fulfillment steps handled by Mobility Ops (no automated provisioning).

---

### **Acceptance Criteria**

* Mobile device catalog is added to Storefront with accurate models, specifications, and availability details.
* Role-based visibility ensures only authorized users or AD groups can view or request mobile devices.
* End-to-end workflow—from request creation to approval routing and handoff to Mobility Ops—is fully validated.
* Fulfillment instructions clearly define manual steps Mobility Ops must follow for device setup and MDM enrollment.
* All UI and API flows for requesting mobile devices are tested for consistency and correctness.

### **Benefit Explanation**

This feature introduces mobile devices into Storefront, enabling a standardized request and approval process. It improves visibility for users, provides clarity for Mobility Ops, and creates a single, organized channel for managing corporate mobile device requests—without relying on automated provisioning.
