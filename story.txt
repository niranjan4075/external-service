### **Description**

Integrate the Shuttle onboarding platform with Storefront to automatically receive new-hire user data. Shuttle is the first step in the GEICO onboarding journey, and Storefront follows as the platform for provisioning devices. This integration will allow Storefront to pull user attributes, roles, and required device information from Shuttle so the device-ordering workflow is pre-populated and triggered accurately for new employees.

---

### **Acceptance Criteria**

* Shuttle API or data feed is integrated with Storefront to retrieve new-hire user details.
* Required fields such as name, email, start date, role, AD group, and location are mapped and stored within Storefront.
* Storefront can identify “new user onboarding” cases and prepare device workflows based on Shuttle data.
* Validation ensures data is synced correctly and duplicates or missing information are handled gracefully.
* End-to-end testing confirms that a new hire completing Shuttle appears in Storefront with correct device eligibility.

---

### **Benefit Explanation**

Integrating Shuttle with Storefront streamlines the new-hire onboarding process by eliminating manual data entry and ensuring accurate, timely device provisioning. This reduces operational overhead, improves the employee onboarding experience, and creates a seamless flow from initial onboarding (Shuttle) to device fulfillment (Storefront).
