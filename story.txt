### Description

Review the existing Storefront certificates for all prod and non-prod environments. If any cert is set to expire before **Nov 30**, generate and install new certificates, update configuration as needed, and validate that Storefront encryption and connectivity continue to work as expected.

---

### Acceptance Criteria

* All Storefront certs in prod and non-prod are reviewed and their expiry dates documented.
* Any certificate expiring before Nov 30 is renewed, installed, and referenced correctly in application and platform configs.
* Storefront services start successfully with the new certificates and no TLS/handshake errors are observed in logs.
* Data encryption and secure communication (mTLS/HTTPS) are validated via functional and smoke tests across environments.
* Stakeholders sign off that there is no customer or internal impact from the cert updates.
