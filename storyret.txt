Description

Perform a discovery spike to analyze and document the Mobile Device workflow. Collaborate with the respective engineering, infrastructure, and business teams to identify dependencies, integration points, and potential improvements required for future implementation.

Acceptance Criteria

Conduct discussions with Operations Engineering, Storefront, and related platform teams to understand current workflow gaps and integration needs.

Document the end-to-end flow of the existing Mobile Device process, including triggers, data exchange, and external service interactions.

Identify pain points, validation gaps, and dependencies across systems.

Provide a summary of findings and recommended next steps for implementation.

Discovery documentation is reviewed and approved by stakeholders before closure.
