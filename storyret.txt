Description (4 lines)

This update ensures executives and ELTs are not directly notified by workflow emails.
All workflow notifications will instead be routed to their designated executive assistants.
This avoids frequent interruptions for executives while keeping assistants informed.
The system must enforce that no email is ever sent directly to executives.

Acceptance Criteria (7 lines)

All workflow-generated emails for executives/ELTs are redirected to their assistants.

Executives/ELTs must never appear in the “To” or “CC” fields of any email.

A configuration mapping must exist between each executive and their assistant’s email.

If a mapping is missing, the system must log an error and skip sending the email.

Redirected emails must include audit logging with original and final recipients.

QA testing must confirm only assistants receive the notifications, never executives.

System must be regression-tested to ensure no existing email workflows are broken.
