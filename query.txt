Great — here's an **optimized set of PromQL queries** for key resource and performance metrics for your `storefront-api-dv.*` pods under the `storefront-dv` namespace and cluster `azure-eastus-st-085-np-aks-001`.

### **1. CPU Usage (cores per pod – smoothed 5m rate):**

```promql
sum by (pod) (
  rate(container_cpu_usage_seconds_total{
    cluster_name="azure-eastus-st-085-np-aks-001",
    namespace="storefront-dv",
    pod=~"storefront-api-dv.*",
    container!="",
    container!="POD"
  }[5m])
)
```
