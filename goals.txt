### **“Smart Triage” – Understand User Intent Before Suggesting Hardware**

#### **Concept:**  
Instead of immediately letting users request physical laptops, **ask why they need a new device**. If it's for short-term or limited-use tasks, suggest a **cloud VM** or **VDI (Virtual Desktop Infrastructure)** instead.

---

### **How It Works:**

1. **Pre-Request Prompt:**  
   Add a simple dropdown or checkbox on the request page:
   > “Why do you need a new device?”  
   - My current device is broken  
   - I need it for testing  
   - I’m onboarding/contractor  
   - I need a high-performance machine temporarily  
   - Other

2. **Smart Suggestion Logic:**  
   Based on their selection, respond with:
   - “Since this is for short-term testing, would you prefer a pre-configured VM instead of a physical laptop?”  
   - “If you're a contractor, a VDI could be provisioned in minutes — skip the shipping wait.”

3. **Integration:**  
   If your company uses platforms like Azure Virtual Desktop, Amazon Workspaces, or VMware Horizon, link the request to auto-provision or redirect the user.

---

### **Benefits:**
- **Cost Savings** – Fewer physical laptops provisioned unnecessarily  
- **Faster Access** – VMs can be spun up in minutes  
- **Security** – Better control for contractors and testers  
- **Sustainability** – Less e-waste and logistics

---

> Design and implement a smart triage system that captures user intent during device requests and recommends a virtual desktop alternative when appropriate. This reduces physical laptop provisioning by at least 15% for short-term and contractor use cases.
