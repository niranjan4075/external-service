**Business Justification**

We are developing a role-based storefront application that streamlines and secures the device request process for employees. When a user logs into the storefront, they are presented with a personalized list of devices that are pre-mapped to them, ensuring relevance and compliance with internal policies.

Upon submitting a request, a Slack notification is automatically sent to the user's manager, identified by their email address, to initiate the approval workflow. This real-time notification mechanism ensures timely manager intervention and avoids delays often associated with traditional approval methods.

Once the manager approves or rejects the request, the decision is automatically logged and submitted to **Remedy**, our internal IT Service Management system, for recordkeeping, ticketing, and further action. This end-to-end automation reduces manual overhead, minimizes the risk of human error, enhances auditability, and accelerates the overall fulfillment process.

This application increases efficiency, accountability, and transparency in device provisioning while aligning with enterprise compliance standards and operational workflows.
