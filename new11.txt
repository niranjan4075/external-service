


### **Question 3 – Overview (Focused on Slack Integration Review):**

The review is for the Slack integration within the role-based storefront application used for device provisioning. This component enables real-time approval workflows by sending interactive Slack bot notifications to the requestor’s manager when a device request is submitted.

The Slack app is configured with the following OAuth scopes to support its functionality:
- `app_mentions:read` – Detect when the app is mentioned to trigger workflows or respond with context.
- `channels:read` – Access metadata about channels to determine appropriate context or delivery options.
- `chat:write` – Post approval messages, alerts, and status updates in Slack channels or direct messages.
- `chat:write.public` – Post messages to public channels even if the app isn't a member.
- `commands` – Enable slash commands for testing or admin-triggered Slack interactions.
- `im:read` – Retrieve metadata about direct messages to the app.
- `im:write` – Send direct messages to users, typically used for reminders or confirmations.
- `team:read` – Access team-level metadata such as domain and workspace information for internal mapping.
- `users:read` – Retrieve user details for matching requestors and managers.
- `users:read.email` – Resolve Slack users by email address to map against enterprise directory and route notifications correctly.

This integration ensures timely manager decision-making, minimizes delays, and provides traceability. All manager responses (Approve/Reject) are securely logged and forwarded to the Remedy platform for ticket generation and downstream fulfillment. This Slack-based workflow is being reviewed for its functionality, data flow, scope usage, and compliance with enterprise security standards.
